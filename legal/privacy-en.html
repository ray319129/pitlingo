Privacy Policy

PitLingo browser extension　·　Last updated: October 31, 2026　·　繁體中文版
Note This is an English translation of our Traditional Chinese privacy policy. If the two versions differ, the Traditional Chinese version prevails.
Summary PitLingo sends the English subtitles shown on screen to our server, translates them into Traditional Chinese, and displays them. If you do not sign in or purchase, we do not know who you are: there is only a random installation ID. We do not record which videos you watch and we do not track you across websites.
Only when you purchase, sign in, or contact us do we receive the email address and other data needed for that action. Each purpose and retention period is listed below.

1. What we collect

1.1 Subtitle text

When you watch a video on F1TV with English subtitles turned on, PitLingo reads the English subtitle text on the page and sends it to our server to be translated into Traditional Chinese.

These translations are stored and shared with other users. A video's subtitles are the same for everyone, so once a line has been translated, nobody else needs to translate it again. This is how the service keeps its cost low. What we store is only the mapping “English sentence → Chinese translation”. It does not include who sent it.

1.2 Installation ID

On first launch we generate a random ID (for example a3f2c1e8-…) and store it in your browser. It is used for three things only:

  • Preventing abuse (a daily translation limit per installation)
  • Staged rollouts (letting a small share of users try new remote settings first)
  • License binding (if you purchased a paid plan)

The ID is random, contains no personal information, and is not linked to your F1TV account. In the following two cases, however, it is recorded together with other data. We tell you this explicitly:

  • When a license is activated (automatically, once you sign in): the ID is added to that license's list of activated devices (with the activation and last-used times) to enforce the per-license device limit. The license itself is associated with the email used at purchase.
  • When you sign in inside the extension: translation requests carry your sign-in credential, and the daily free allowance is counted per account (clearing browser data does not reset it). We record only “how many lines this account used today”, not which videos.

You can change this ID at any time by removing and reinstalling the extension.

1.3 Usage statistics

We collect aggregate usage statistics to improve the product and its pricing. All of them are counts and contain nothing that identifies you:

What we recordWhy
Session type (practice / qualifying / sprint / race / other)To judge whether the free tier is reasonable
Minutes watched when the free allowance ran out (in buckets, such as “10–15 min”)To judge whether 15 minutes is enough
Whether the user is a paying user (yes / no)To understand conversion
Extension version and error typeTo detect problems caused by a new version

1.4 Diagnostic reports (only when you send one)

When you click “Send diagnostics” in the extension, we receive:

  • The extension's operating state: version, cache hit rate, and an event timeline
  • The URL of the video you are watching (authorization data in the URL is masked)
  • The problem description and contact details you choose to enter (both optional)

Diagnostic reports do not contain your license code or any access token. They are used only to reproduce and fix problems and are deleted automatically after 90 days. A copy is also emailed to our support inbox (see “Resend” and “support inbox” in Section 5).

1.5 Account sign-in

The “My account” page lets you see your purchases, licenses and activated devices. You can purchase and use the free allowance without signing in; a license, however, works only when the extension is signed in to the account of the email used at purchase (licenses are bound to an account and activate automatically once you sign in). There are two ways to sign in:

MethodData we receive and keep
Sign in with Google Your Google account ID, email, display name, and the account creation and last sign-in times. We do not receive your profile photo, contacts, Drive, Gmail content or any other Google data. Google performs the authentication; we never receive your Google password.
Email verification code We email a 6-digit code to you. The server stores only a hash of the code, which expires after 10 minutes. After sign-in we keep your email and the account creation and last sign-in times.

After you sign in, the website keeps your session in one strictly necessary cookie: it is HttpOnly (scripts on the page cannot read it), sent only over encrypted connections and only to our API, and valid for 14 days. The extension keeps its sign-in credential in the extension's own storage, valid for 30 days. When you click “Sign out”, the server revokes that credential immediately; “Sign out of all devices” revokes the credentials of all your browsers and extensions. We do not use any advertising or tracking cookies. If you start the sign-in from the extension, the website hands the credential to the extension through a one-time pairing code that is valid for 5 minutes and deleted as soon as it is used.

If you purchased with a different email, you can “claim” that purchase on the account page by entering the email used at purchase: we email a verification code to the email used at purchase (nothing is sent if that email has no purchases), and only after you enter it is that email added to your account's list of merged addresses.

Transferring a complimentary race-week pass: the recipient email you enter on the account page becomes the owner of that license. We send that person a notification email and keep “transferred to whom” in your order record for customer support and refunds. Please enter only the email of someone who has agreed to receive the transfer.

Welcome email: when you sign in for the first time and your account is created, we send one welcome email to the sign-in address, describing how to use PitLingo and our plans (with a purchase link). It is the only one; we do not send marketing emails afterwards.

New-device sign-in alerts: when your account signs in on a new browser or a new extension installation, we email you (device type, approximate location, time) so you can immediately “sign out of all devices” if it was not you. Devices added within 30 minutes of creating the account (for example, connecting the extension right after signing up) do not trigger an alert. To recognize a “new” device, the website sets a device-recognition cookie in your browser (randomly generated, HttpOnly, valid for 400 days, not deleted on sign-out), and your account data keeps up to 20 previously used devices as a hashed identifier, device type (for example “Windows Chrome”) and sign-in time. The approximate location in the alert is estimated by Cloudflare from the connection (city and country); it is written only into that email and not stored elsewhere, and the IP address is not stored.

1.6 Payment information

We never see or store your card number or any payment details. Payment is completed entirely on the pages of our payment provider, ECPay (綠界科技). At checkout we pass the order number, amount, product name and the email you entered to ECPay to create the transaction, and receive ECPay's notification of whether the order was paid. For ATM or convenience-store payments we also receive the payment account or code generated by ECPay, to show it to you on the payment result page. The email you enter at checkout is used only to send receipts and activation notices. It is not used for marketing. (The one-time welcome email sent when an account is created is described in section 1.5.)

1.7 Orders and cart

The plans, quantities, discount details and amount due that you choose on the purchase page are stored together with the order number and email to activate licenses, look up orders and handle refund disputes. An order is created at checkout (including orders that are never paid). The upgrade feature uses your signed-in account (and the emails you have claimed into it) to look up the amount paid for earlier race-week passes — nothing is looked up or credited when you are not signed in; it looks up only amounts and validity periods, never what you watched.

1.8 F1TV subscription purchasing service

If you buy our F1TV subscription purchasing service, we also receive what is necessary to provide it:

  • Your email — to complete the subscription and to notify you to sign in and view it
  • The order's processing status (pending / completed), delivery time and whether the notification email was sent, for customer support
  • The sign-in email and password of the F1TV account we provide for your use (see below)

We never ask for the password of your own F1TV account. For an F1TV account we provide for your use, its sign-in details are stored encrypted and decrypted only when you sign in to “My account” and click “Show password”; every view is logged with the time and device type (computer / mobile). The password is never included in our emails. The sign-in details are deleted automatically when the usage period ends. This is a manual service, and the information above is handled by a person.

1.9 Contact form

When you submit the “Contact us” form, we store the email, order number (optional) and problem description you enter and create a ticket number. This data is used only to reply to you and is deleted automatically after 90 days.

2. What we do not collect

  • We do not record which video you watch. The video ID is used as a cache key for translations, but it is never stored together with your installation ID, license code or account — the service cannot reconstruct any user's viewing history from the data it stores. (The only exception is a diagnostic report you choose to send, which contains the current video URL; see 1.4.)
  • We do not store IP addresses. To prevent the free allowance from being claimed repeatedly and to rate-limit forms and sign-in attempts, we apply a one-way hash (HMAC-SHA256) to your IP address and use the first 16 characters. The raw IP address is never written to storage; the hash cannot be turned back into an IP address and cannot be matched to your license code or email. Free-allowance records expire daily and rate-limit records within 2 minutes; attempt counters kept to stop verification codes from being brute-forced (hashed IP and email, never the code itself) are deleted after at most 3 days.
  • We do not read your browsing history, bookmarks, passwords or other tabs.
  • We do not read your F1TV account information or cookies.
  • We do not use advertising trackers, nor analytics tools such as Google Analytics that build personal browsing histories. The website (pitlingo.com) uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count overall page views and page-load speed (see Section 5): according to Cloudflare, it does not use cookies or browser storage such as localStorage and does not identify individuals by IP address or browser information. Cloudflare adds it when serving the website; it is not part of our code, and the extension contains no analytics tools at all.
  • We do not track you across websites.
  • We do not sell, rent or exchange any data.

6. Your rights

Under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act, you have the right to inquire about, review, correct and delete the personal data we hold, and to ask us to stop collecting, processing or using it.

The service is designed to minimize the personal data it collects. The personal data we may actually hold is: the email and order records from your purchases, the email, display name and Google account ID from signing in, and the contact details and content you provide through the contact form or a diagnostic report. Please send requests to pitlingo.office@gmail.com.

7. Children's privacy

The service is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

8. Governing law and jurisdiction

This policy is governed by the laws of the Republic of China (Taiwan). Any dispute arising from this policy shall be submitted to the Taiwan Taichung District Court as the court of first instance.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. If the change materially affects how data is collected, we will notify you in the extension.
